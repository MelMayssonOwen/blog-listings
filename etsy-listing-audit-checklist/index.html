Etsy listing audit checklist: 20 points, ten minutes

To audit an Etsy listing, check three things in order: does the title and do the 13 tags match phrases real buyers type; is every attribute and category filled; and does the first photo plus price earn the click and the sale (because Etsy's listing-quality signal is driven by clicks and conversions). Work top to bottom through the 20 points below. The whole pass takes about ten minutes per listing and needs no paid tool.

The checklist

Title & tags (query matching)

  1. Does the first part of the title contain the exact phrase a buyer would search (not a clever brand name)?
  2. Are the most important keywords in the first 40 characters (what shows in search results)?
  3. Are all 13 tags used? Empty tag slots are wasted ranking surface.
  4. Are tags multi-word phrases ("personalized dog collar") rather than single words ("dog")?
  5. Do tags avoid repeating the exact same phrase, and instead cover synonyms and long-tail variants?
  6. Do the title and tags reinforce each other (Etsy weighs both)?

Attributes & category

  1. Is the most specific category selected?
  2. Is every available attribute (color, material, occasion, size) filled? Etsy uses attributes in search and filters.
  3. Do attributes match the actual product (mismatches hurt quality signals)?

Photos & the click

  1. Is the first photo clear, well-lit, and understandable as a thumbnail?
  2. Are all 10 image slots used, including scale, detail, and in-use shots?
  3. Is there a video? Listings with video tend to hold attention.

Conversion (the quality signal)

  1. Does the first line of the description restate what the item is and who it is for?
  2. Is pricing competitive for the category (check comparable listings)?
  3. Are shipping cost and processing time reasonable? Buyers filter on these.
  4. Does the listing have reviews, and are you asking happy buyers for them?
  5. Is the item marked with accurate renewal/availability so it stays live?

Housekeeping

  1. Are you using all relevant occasion/holiday attributes for seasonal demand?
  2. Have you checked Etsy Shop Stats to see which search terms already bring visits?
  3. Have you edited stale listings recently (freshness plus updated keywords)?

Why this order

Etsy's Etsy Help Center: "How Etsy Search Works" documentation describes ranking as query matching first (title, tags, attributes), then listing quality (how often shoppers click and buy), plus recency and a few shop-level factors. The checklist follows that logic: match the query, then make the listing convert. A keyword tool can speed up steps 1, 5, but the audit itself is free and you can run it today. Frequently asked questions

How do I audit an Etsy listing?
Check query matching first (title and all 13 tags match real buyer phrases), then completeness (category and every attribute filled), then conversion (first photo, price, shipping, reviews). Etsy ranks on query match plus listing quality, so audit in that order.
How many tags should an Etsy listing use?
All 13. Empty tag slots waste ranking surface. Use multi-word phrases and synonyms rather than single words or repeats of the same phrase.
Do I need a tool to audit my Etsy listing?
No. This 20-point pass is free and takes about ten minutes. A keyword tool like eRank can speed up finding the phrases for the title and tags, but the audit itself needs nothing but Etsy's own search bar and your Shop Stats.
