Etsy & Amazon listing optimization: buyer FAQ

The most common questions from marketplace sellers: you need only one of Helium 10 or Jungle Scout, not both; no tool can guarantee rankings; listing changes surface in days but ranking gains follow conversion, not the edit alone; eRank is the best-value Etsy tool; and you should fix your organic listing before spending on ads. Full answers below, each grounded in Etsy's or Amazon's own documentation where relevant.

These are the questions sellers ask before buying a tool, answered plainly. For the tool comparisons behind them, see Etsy tools and Amazon tools.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Helium 10 or Jungle Scout, or both?
Just one. They overlap almost completely on Amazon product research, keyword research, and rank tracking. Helium 10 has a free plan and starts at $129/month; Jungle Scout has no free trial and sells three Catalyst tiers. Buying both is redundant for nearly all sellers.
Do listing tools guarantee better rankings?
No. No tool or service can guarantee a marketplace ranking, because Etsy and Amazon control their algorithms and results depend on execution and competition. Reputable tools, including Listingtonic, state this plainly.
How fast do listing changes show up?
It varies. Etsy re-indexes edited listings and gives recently changed or renewed listings a short recency boost, so changes can surface within days, but ranking gains follow only if the listing also converts. On Amazon, indexing of new keywords is usually quick, but rank movement depends on sales and relevance over time. Neither is instant.
Which Etsy tool is worth paying for: eRank, Marmalead, Alura, or EverBee?
eRank is the best value (free plan, then $5.99/month). Alura is best if you want an all-in-one with a listing optimizer and A/B testing ($7.99 to $29.99/month). EverBee is best for product and sales research (free, then $29.99/month). Marmalead ($19/month, no free plan) suits sellers who want seasonality scoring.
Should I run ads or fix organic listings first?
Fix the organic listing first. Ads send traffic to a page that still has to convert; if the title, images, and price are not optimized, you pay for clicks that do not buy. Optimize the listing, confirm it converts, then amplify with ads.
What is the difference between a research tool and a rewrite tool?
A research tool (eRank, Helium 10, Jungle Scout) finds keywords and demand data. A rewrite tool takes that and produces the actual title, bullets, and description copy. On Amazon, Listingtonic is a rewrite tool ($199 one-time); on Etsy, the rewrite is manual. Most sellers need both steps but often buy only the research half.
Is there a free way to optimize my listings?
Yes. Etsy's Shop Stats and search autocomplete are free and show real buyer queries. Amazon's Product Opportunity Explorer and Manage Your Experiments (A/B testing) are free in Seller Central. Combined with a manual audit, you can optimize without paying for any third-party tool.
