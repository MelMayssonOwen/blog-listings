Etsy and Amazon listing optimization, explained by someone who reads the docs

Listing optimization means editing a product's title, tags, images, bullets, and description so the marketplace's search engine can match it to buyer queries and so shoppers actually click and convert. On Etsy the levers are the title, the 13 tags, attributes, and a listing-quality signal driven by clicks and sales; on Amazon they are the title, five bullets, backend search terms, images, and A+ Content. The fastest wins are almost always fixing an existing listing, not buying a new tool.

The one idea to take away

Keyword-research tools (eRank, Marmalead, Alura, EverBee, Helium 10, Jungle Scout) tell you which words to target. They do not write the listing. Rewriting the title, tags, and bullets to actually use those words, in language buyers search, is a separate job. Most sellers over-invest in research subscriptions and under-invest in the rewrite. Both matter; the rewrite is where conversion moves.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a paid tool to optimize my listings?
No. The highest-impact edits are free: matching your title and tags to how buyers actually search, filling every Etsy attribute, and rewriting Amazon bullets around buyer objections. Paid keyword tools speed up research but do not rewrite the listing for you.
Etsy or Amazon: are the rules the same?
The principle is the same (match the query, then earn the click and sale) but the mechanics differ. Etsy weighs the title, 13 tags, attributes, and a listing-quality score. Amazon weighs the title, five bullets, backend search terms, images, and A+ Content. Do not copy an Etsy title straight onto Amazon or vice versa.
