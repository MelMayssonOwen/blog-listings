Best Etsy SEO tools for 2026 (ranked by what job they do)

The best Etsy SEO tool for most sellers in 2026 is eRank, because it has a usable free plan and a $5.99/month Basic tier. Alura is the best all-in-one (research plus a listing optimizer and A/B testing), EverBee is the best for product and sales-estimate research, and Marmalead is the pick if you specifically want its seasonality and engagement scoring. But the single most valuable 'tool' is free: Etsy's own search and Shop Stats, read against the ranking factors Etsy publishes.

The ranked list

  1. eRank, best overall value. Free plan (5 lookups/day), then Basic $5.99/mo, Pro $9.99/mo, Expert $29.99/mo. Deep keyword data, tag ideas, and listing audits.
  2. Alura, best all-in-one. Free plan, then $7.99, $29.99/mo. Adds a listing optimizer, buyer follow-ups, and A/B testing on top of keyword research.
  3. EverBee, best for product/sales research. Free Hobby tier, then $29.99/mo Growth. Estimated sales, revenue, and conversion data.
  4. Marmalead, best for seasonality read. No free plan; $19/mo, $190/yr, or $300 lifetime. Engagement and seasonality scoring.
  5. Etsy Shop Stats + Search bar, best free signal. Etsy's own search autocomplete and your Shop Stats show real buyer queries and which listings convert. Free, and closest to the source of truth.

Full pricing and side-by-side detail are on our eRank vs Marmalead vs Alura vs EverBee page.

How to choose

Pick by the job, not the brand. If you need keywords, eRank's free tier is enough to start. If you need to research products and demand, EverBee's sales estimates are the differentiator. If you want research plus a listing optimizer and A/B tests in one subscription, Alura is the most complete. If you sell year-round and want to read seasonal demand, Marmalead's scoring is its reason to exist.

The step every tool skips

None of these writes your listing. After the research, you still have to rewrite the title and 13 tags into the exact phrases buyers type, fill every attribute, and make the first photo earn the click. Etsy's Etsy Seller Handbook and the Etsy Help Center: "How Etsy Search Works" article both stress that listing quality, clicks and sales, feeds ranking, so the rewrite is not optional. Frequently asked questions

What is the best free Etsy SEO tool?
eRank's free plan is the best free dedicated tool, with 5 keyword lookups per day and listing audits. Etsy's own search autocomplete and Shop Stats are also free and show real buyer queries.
Is eRank worth it in 2026?
For most sellers, yes: the free plan is usable and Basic is only $5.99/month, cheaper than Marmalead or EverBee's paid tiers. Whether you need to pay at all depends on how much keyword research you do.
Do professional Etsy sellers use these tools?
Many do, but the tools are aids, not autopilot. The ranking work Etsy rewards, matching real queries and converting clicks to sales, still comes down to how you write and photograph the listing.
