What Do You Mean by Keyword Research?

Published 2026-11-15

TL;DR: Keyword research is the process of finding the words and phrases shoppers use to search for a product, then choosing the ones that best match the item and buyer intent. For Etsy titles, it helps sellers use shopper language, keep titles clear, and choose the most relevant terms without stuffing in every possible phrase.

Keyword research

Keyword research means identifying the terms and phrases buyers enter when looking for a product, then selecting the ones that align most closely with the item and purchase intent. For Etsy titles, that involves using the wording shoppers really type so the title is more precise and pertinent. Etsy’s own guidance on titles and search visibility should be the final referee here, so check the relevant help docs before you decide. Start with Etsy’s help pages on listing titles and search visibility: https://help.etsy.com/

How keyword research works for a listing

Start with the product itself. Note the likely search terms, compare them with how buyers would phrase the item, then keep the terms that are specific and accurate. The job is not to brainstorm every possible phrase and force it into the title. It is to find the wording that fits the item and the way people search for it.

What makes a good keyword for Etsy titles

A good Etsy keyword is relevant to the exact item, clear to shoppers, and aligned with what the listing actually sells. If the phrase sounds attractive but does not describe the product, it is not helping. Etsy’s own guidance on titles and search visibility should govern title decisions, since the platform decides how titles are interpreted and shown.

Worked example: turning a product into search terms

Imagine a handmade item: a ceramic spoon rest with a speckled glaze. A raw brainstorm might look like this:

  • spoon rest
  • handmade spoon rest
  • ceramic spoon rest
  • kitchen gift
  • speckled glaze spoon rest
  • pottery spoon holder
  • rustic kitchen accessory

That is a normal first pass. Now narrow it to what a buyer would actually search for and what the item really is. The broad phrase “kitchen gift” is too vague on its own, while “ceramic spoon rest” and “handmade ceramic spoon rest” are specific and accurate. “Pottery spoon holder” may fit if that wording matches the piece, but only if the item is truly made that way. The point is to keep the terms that describe the item honestly and help the right buyer recognize it quickly.

A hypothetical finished title could look like this:

  • Handmade Ceramic Spoon Rest, Speckled Glaze Pottery Kitchen Accessory

That is very different from the brainstorm. The brainstorm is a cloud of possibilities. The title is the version that survives contact with reality.

How keyword research connects to title length

This is where keyword research ties back to the bigger title question. When a title has to stay concise, keyword research helps decide which terms deserve the space. The goal is not to use every keyword. That is how titles turn into a word salad with a side of confusion. The goal is to prioritize the terms most likely to help the right buyer understand the listing quickly.
