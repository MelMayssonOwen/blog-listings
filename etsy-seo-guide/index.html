Etsy SEO: how Etsy search actually ranks your listings

Etsy SEO is the practice of matching your listing to buyer searches so Etsy's search engine ranks it well. Etsy's own documentation says ranking comes down to query matching (how well your title, tags, and attributes match what a shopper typed), listing quality (how often shoppers click and buy your listing), recency, a customer-and-market-experience score, shipping price, and translations/language. You control the first two most directly: write the title and 13 tags in real buyer phrases, fill every attribute, and make the listing convert.

The ranking factors Etsy names

According to the Etsy Help Center: "How Etsy Search Works" documentation and the Etsy Seller Handbook, Etsy weighs, roughly:

  • Query matching — the words in your title, tags, categories, and attributes versus the words a buyer searched.
  • Listing quality — how often shoppers who see your listing click and then purchase it. This is the conversion signal.
  • Recency — new and recently renewed/edited listings get a temporary visibility boost.
  • Customer & market experience score — shop-level factors like reviews, a completed About section, and clear policies.
  • Shipping price — listings with reasonable (or free) shipping are favored, and buyers filter on it.
  • Translations & language — providing your own titles/tags in the buyer's language rather than relying on auto-translation.

What this means in practice

Because query matching and listing quality dominate, two moves matter most: (1) put the exact phrases buyers search into your title and all 13 tags, and (2) make the listing convert with a strong first photo, competitive price, and reasonable shipping so your listing-quality signal climbs. Everything else (recency, shop score, shipping) is supporting.

Title and tags: the mechanics

Etsy reads the title and tags together. Use multi-word phrases, cover synonyms across your 13 tags rather than repeating one phrase, and lead the title with the phrase most buyers actually type. Attributes (color, material, occasion) are also matched in search and power the left-hand filters, so fill every one. Our Etsy listing audit checklist turns this into a step-by-step pass.

Tools that help (and the step they skip)

eRank, Marmalead, Alura, and EverBee speed up finding those phrases and reading demand; see the comparison. But none rewrites the listing — matching the query and earning the click is still your writing and your photography. That is the work Etsy's quality signal rewards.

Frequently asked questions

How does Etsy search work?
Etsy ranks on query matching (title, tags, attributes vs the buyer's search), listing quality (click and purchase rate), recency, a shop-level customer and market experience score, shipping price, and language/translations. Query matching and listing quality matter most.
What are the most important Etsy SEO factors?
Query matching and listing quality. Put real buyer phrases in your title and all 13 tags, fill every attribute, and make the listing convert with a strong first photo, fair price, and reasonable shipping.
Do Etsy tags still matter in 2026?
Yes. Etsy matches the words in your 13 tags (and title and attributes) against buyer searches. Use all 13 as multi-word phrases covering synonyms, not single words or repeats.
