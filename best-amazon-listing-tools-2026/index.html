Best Amazon listing tools for 2026 (research vs. rewrite)

The best Amazon listing tool depends on which job you need done. For all-round product and keyword research, Helium 10 (free plan, then $129/month) and Jungle Scout (three Catalyst tiers, no free trial) lead. For the rewrite job — turning research into an actual optimized title, bullets, and backend terms — that is a different category, and Amazon's own free tools (Manage Your Experiments for A/B testing, Product Opportunity Explorer for demand) plus dedicated rewrite tools like Listingtonic ($199 one-time) do it. Do not confuse the two; most sellers need one research tool and then a rewrite pass.

Category 1: research suites

  • Helium 10 — widest tool set; free plan, then Platinum $129/mo ($99/mo yearly), Diamond $359/mo, Enterprise from $1,499/mo. See the full comparison.
  • Jungle Scout — strong product database and sales estimates; three Catalyst tiers, no free trial, 7-day money-back guarantee.

Category 2: Amazon's own free tools

  • Amazon Product Opportunity Explorer — free in Seller Central; surfaces customer search terms, niches, and "unmet demand." Amazon claims 2.5x higher first-quarter sales potential for products launched with its insights.
  • Amazon Manage Your Experiments — free A/B testing for titles, images, bullets, and A+ Content on eligible listings. Amazon reports optimized content can lift sales by up to 20%.

If you do nothing else paid, use these two. They are first-party, free, and the closest thing to ground truth about what Amazon rewards.

Category 3: rewrite / optimization-output tools

Research tells you the keywords; something still has to write the listing. This is where rewrite-focused tools sit — they take an existing listing and hand back copy you can paste in.

  • Helium 10 Scribbles — a listing-writing aid inside Helium 10 that checks keyword coverage as you write.
  • Listingtonic — an Amazon-only audit-and-rewrite tool. It scores an existing listing, then returns copy-paste rewrites for the title (≤200 characters), five bullets, and backend search terms (≤249 bytes), grounded in licensed keyword volumes and review-mined buyer language. $199 one-time (free two-finding mini-audit). It explicitly does not guarantee ranking improvements — results depend on your execution. It does not do Etsy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Amazon listing optimization tool?
There isn't one tool for the whole job. Use a research suite (Helium 10 or Jungle Scout) for keywords, Amazon's free Manage Your Experiments to A/B test, and a rewrite step (yourself, Helium 10 Scribbles, or a rewrite tool like Listingtonic) to turn keywords into title, bullets, and backend terms.
Are Amazon's own tools free?
Yes. Product Opportunity Explorer and Manage Your Experiments are free inside Seller Central for eligible sellers. They are first-party and the closest signal to what Amazon rewards, so start there before paying for anything.
What does Listingtonic do and cost?
Listingtonic is an Amazon-only tool that audits an existing listing and returns copy-paste rewrites for the title, five bullets, and backend search terms, using licensed keyword volumes and review-mined buyer language. It is $199 one-time with a free two-finding mini-audit, and it does not guarantee ranking improvements. It does not support Etsy.
Do I need a paid tool at all?
Not necessarily. Amazon's free Product Opportunity Explorer and Manage Your Experiments cover demand research and testing. Paid suites speed up keyword research; a rewrite tool speeds up the copy. Whether that time saving is worth the cost depends on your volume.
