Helium 10 alternatives for Amazon sellers

The main Helium 10 alternative is Jungle Scout, a comparable research suite with a strong product database (three Catalyst tiers, no free trial, 7-day money-back guarantee). Amazon's own free tools, Product Opportunity Explorer for demand research and Manage Your Experiments for A/B testing, replace much of what sellers pay Helium 10 for. And for the rewrite job specifically (turning keywords into a finished title, bullets, and backend terms), a dedicated rewrite tool like Listingtonic ($199 one-time) is an alternative to Helium 10's Scribbles. Pick by the job you actually need.

The honest breakdown

Jungle Scout is the closest like-for-like: same core jobs (product research, keyword research, rank tracking), different interface, and a reputation for accurate sales estimates. See the head-to-head. The catch is no free plan or trial, only a 7-day money-back guarantee.

Amazon's free tools are the alternative most sellers overlook. Amazon Product Opportunity Explorer gives you demand and search-term data straight from Amazon (which claims 2.5x higher first-quarter sales potential for products launched with its insights), and Amazon Manage Your Experiments handles A/B testing (Amazon says optimized content can lift sales up to 20%). If budget is the reason you are leaving Helium 10, start here.

For the rewrite step, Helium 10's value is partly Scribbles, its writing aid. A dedicated rewrite tool like Listingtonic (Amazon-only, $199 one-time) is an alternative for that one job, returning a finished title, bullets, and backend terms rather than checking coverage as you type. It has no research suite, so it replaces Scribbles, not all of Helium 10.

Bottom line

There is no single drop-in replacement, because Helium 10 bundles several jobs. Match the alternative to the job: Jungle Scout for a full suite, Amazon's free tools for research and testing on a budget, and a rewrite tool for the copy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Helium 10?
Jungle Scout is the closest full-suite alternative. But Amazon's own free Product Opportunity Explorer and Manage Your Experiments replace much of Helium 10's research and testing value, and a rewrite tool covers the copy step. There is no single drop-in because Helium 10 bundles several jobs.
Is there a free alternative to Helium 10?
Amazon's Product Opportunity Explorer (demand research) and Manage Your Experiments (A/B testing) are free in Seller Central and cover a lot of what sellers pay Helium 10 for. Helium 10 itself also has a limited free plan.
What replaces Helium 10 for writing the listing?
For the rewrite job specifically, a dedicated tool like Listingtonic (Amazon only, $199 one-time) returns a finished title, bullets, and backend terms, an alternative to Helium 10's Scribbles writing aid. It does not replace the research suite.
