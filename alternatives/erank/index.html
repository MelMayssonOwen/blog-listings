eRank alternatives: the best Etsy tools if eRank isn't for you

The best eRank alternatives for Etsy sellers are Alura (free plan, then $7.99, $29.99/month, adds a listing optimizer and A/B testing), EverBee (free Hobby tier, then $29.99/month, strong sales estimates), and Marmalead ($19/month, seasonality and engagement scoring). For most sellers eRank remains the best value because of its free tier and $5.99/month Basic plan, but Alura is the better pick if you want an all-in-one, and EverBee if you prioritize product and sales research. Etsy's own free Shop Stats is the alternative that costs nothing.

Which alternative fits which seller

Want an all-in-one? Alura is the closest, bundling research with a listing optimizer and A/B testing at $14.99/month (Growth). Doing product research? EverBee's sales estimates are its edge. Selling seasonal goods? Marmalead's seasonality view is built for that. On a zero budget? Etsy's own Shop Stats and search autocomplete show real demand for free.

A note for cross-marketplace sellers

If you are looking at eRank because you also sell on Amazon, note that these Etsy tools won't cover Amazon's title/bullet/backend-term structure. For the Amazon side, see best Amazon listing tools; the rewrite-focused option there, Listingtonic, is Amazon-only and does not replace eRank for Etsy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to eRank?
Alura is the best all-in-one alternative (research plus a listing optimizer and A/B testing from $7.99/month). EverBee is best for product and sales research, and Marmalead for seasonality. eRank itself remains the best value for pure keyword work.
Is there a free alternative to eRank?
Yes. Alura and EverBee both have free plans, and Etsy's own Shop Stats and search autocomplete are free and show the real search terms bringing you traffic.
Why switch from eRank?
Switch if you want features eRank lacks: Alura for a built-in listing optimizer and A/B testing, EverBee for sales estimates, or Marmalead for seasonality scoring. If you only need keyword data, eRank's value is hard to beat.
