Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout: do you need both?

No, you do not need both. Helium 10 and Jungle Scout do the same core jobs for Amazon sellers: product research, keyword research, and listing/rank tracking. Pick one. Helium 10 has a free plan and starts at $129/month (Platinum), or $99/month billed yearly; Jungle Scout has no free plan or free trial, only a 7-day money-back guarantee, and sells three Catalyst tiers. Choose Helium 10 if you want a free tier and the widest tool set; choose Jungle Scout if you prefer its cleaner product database and sales-estimate accuracy. Neither one rewrites your listing.

What each is good at

Helium 10 is the broader Swiss-army suite: Black Box (product research), Magnet and Cerebro (keyword research), Frankenstein (keyword processing), Scribbles (listing writing aid), plus rank tracking and refund tools. The free plan lets you try limited uses; paid starts at Platinum ($129/month, or $99/month billed annually = $1,188/year), then Diamond ($359/month, $279 yearly), then Enterprise from $1,499/month billed annually.

Jungle Scout is tighter and research-led: its product database and sales estimates have a strong reputation, and Catalyst bundles product research, keyword research, and listing/rank tools across three tiers (Starter, Growth Accelerator, Brand Owner). There is no free plan and no free trial, only a 7-day money-back guarantee. Extra user seats are $49/month.

The gap both leave

Both suites tell you which keywords to target and roughly how a listing ranks. Neither rewrites your title, five bullets, and backend search terms into buyer language for you, that is a separate, manual step, and it is where Amazon says conversion actually moves. Amazon's own Amazon Manage Your Experiments tool reports that "optimized content can help increase sales by up to 20%," and Amazon Product Opportunity Explorer claims products launched with its insights have "2.5x higher sales potential" in their first three months. Those gains come from the rewrite and the test, not from owning a research subscription. Frequently asked questions

Do I need both Helium 10 and Jungle Scout?
No. They overlap almost entirely on product research, keyword research, and rank tracking. Pick one. Paying for both is redundant for nearly all sellers.
Is Helium 10 cheaper than Jungle Scout?
Helium 10 has a free plan and paid plans from $129/month (or $99/month billed yearly). Jungle Scout has no free plan or free trial and sells three Catalyst tiers, so the entry cost depends on the tier; both are comparable at the mid range.
Does Helium 10 or Jungle Scout have a free trial?
Helium 10 offers a limited free plan. Jungle Scout offers neither a free plan nor a free trial, only a 7-day money-back guarantee on standard Catalyst plans.
Do these tools optimize my Amazon listing automatically?
No. They provide keyword and competitor data. Rewriting the title, bullets, and backend search terms is a separate step. Amazon's own A/B testing tool reports optimized content can lift sales by up to 20%, but the rewrite is manual.
